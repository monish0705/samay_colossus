# Manual SAMAY walkthrough

The timed walkthrough, Judge Mode, guided-tour controls and example-loading buttons
have been removed. Use the ordinary farmer interface.

1. Open `/advisor`. In **Crop and cash**, select your crop/market and enter harvest
   quantity, your buyer quotation and required net cash. The dated wholesale reference
   remains a reference, and never fills your buyer quotation automatically.
2. In **Storage and costs**, choose whether you can safely store produce. With no
   storage, skip holding inputs. With storage, enter capacity, a safe holding period,
   daily storage costs and your estimated quantity loss. Check transport and commission.
   **More options** contains the remaining fees, lot sizes, storage assumptions and
   optional price/weather scenarios. Crop defaults are estimates requiring local review.
3. In **Review your plan**, inspect the inputs and costs, acknowledge your assumptions,
   then calculate through the backend. Future prices and holding proceeds remain uncertain.

On Results, compare the selected split, immediate cash, downside, later proceeds and
sell-all-now alternative. Switch English/Kannada to verify unchanged numbers. Return to
inputs to compare zero storage, different cash needs or an adverse user price scenario.

Saved forecast and data availability are read from the backend. Unsupported dates remain
unavailable; a crop planning limit is not a guarantee of storage suitability or model support.

For sale requests, use **Sell to This Mandi** and follow the account/mandi approval flow
in [SALE-WORKFLOW.md](SALE-WORKFLOW.md). No operator accounts or completed transactions
are fabricated for this walkthrough.
