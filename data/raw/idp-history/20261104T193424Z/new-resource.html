APMC Arrivals And Prices (New Source)

URL: https://ckandev.indiadataportal.com/dataset/1749d812-35b4-4635-ac29-dd3116ed31b0/resource/078fd1ea-dab6-4aca-aed9-3a4b8b2c761d/download/apmc-arrivals-and-prices-new-source.csv

The Agricultural Marketing Information Network (AGMARKNET) dataset provides daily price and arrival information for more than 350 different commodities and over 1000 varieties of agricultural produce from wholesale markets across India. This comprehensive dataset is collected from Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMCs) and covers 3231 markets as of May 31, 2026.

The data is uploaded to the AGMARKNET portal, which provides easy access to commodity- and variety-wise daily price and arrival information. This information can be used to analyse the dynamics of agricultural markets, identify patterns and trends, and inform policymaking. The dataset is a valuable resource for researchers, policymakers, and market participants who need to understand the challenges facing India's agricultural sector, which plays a vital role in the country's economy and food security.
